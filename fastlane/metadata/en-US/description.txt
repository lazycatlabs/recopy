Copy once, paste anytime.

ReCopy is a clipboard history that lives in your menu bar. Press ⇧⌘V anywhere, find what you copied, and paste it straight back into the app you were in.

KNOWS WHAT YOU COPIED
Text, links, e-mail addresses, colours (HEX, RGB, HSL), images and files, each with its own icon. Filter by type or by the day it was copied.

YOUR HISTORY, YOUR FOLDER
History is saved in a folder you choose. Put it in iCloud Drive, Google Drive or OneDrive to share it between your Macs. No account, no server.

FEATURES
• Link previews with the site's icon, title and image
• Text in copied screenshots is recognised on your Mac, so you can search for it
• Pin entries to keep them at the top
• Sections by time: Today, Yesterday, This Week, This Month and by month
• Details: source app, characters and words, image size, how often it was copied and pasted
• Annotate, crop and rotate images
• Actions per type: open a link, write an e-mail, copy a colour as HEX, RGB or HSL, save to a file
• Import from Raycast

PRIVATE BY DEFAULT
Passwords that apps mark as secret are never recorded, and you can ignore whole apps. Passwords and Keychain Access are ignored from the start.

YOURS TO TUNE
Change the shortcut, keep history from a day to forever, text size, copy and paste sounds, and open at login.

Pasting straight into other apps needs the Accessibility permission. Without it, ReCopy copies the entry and you press ⌘V yourself.
